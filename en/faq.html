What is t1.mushafuk.online?

This address holds the Urdu, Bengali, Persian, and Indonesian material. The other translations are on t.mushafuk.online.

How many books are here?

There are 10 works: Quran translations and translated tafsirs.

How many pages are here?

There are 51,096 reading pages. A Quran translation is the mushaf’s 604 pages. A translated tafsir is one page for each of the 6,236 verses.

Which works, and how many pages?

  • Quran translations: Persian (Rowwad) and Urdu (Junagarhi) — 2 works, 604 pages each.
  • al-Mukhtasar: Bengali, Persian, and Indonesian — 3 languages, 6,236 verse pages each.
  • al-Sa'di: Persian, Indonesian, and Urdu — 3 languages, 6,236 verse pages each.
  • Ibn Kathir: Bengali and Urdu — 2 languages, 6,236 verse pages each.

How do I open a page?

A translation address is the language and the mushaf page, for example /en/translation/page/1/. A translated tafsir address is the book, the language, the surah, and the verse, for example /tafsir/tercume/mukhtasar/en/2/255/.

How does this relate to the main site?

The mushaf and the general questions are on mushafuk.online. This address is the translation part of that network.
